🎓 Case Study: Student Lead Generation & Institute Conversions

Educational institutes often struggle with high drop-off rates from lead form to actual enrollment. I engineered a two-stage performance strategy built on targeted Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) combined with a structured consultation funnel.

  • Step 1: Hyper-Targeted Meta Lead Campaigns — Designed visual lead ads targeting students interested in skill development, career upgrades, e-commerce, and digital skills.
  • Step 2: Instant Lead Capture & Nurturing — Utilized instant lead forms and custom landing pages to collect verified contact details while interest was highest.
  • Step 3: 1-on-1 Personalized Consultations — Conducted one-on-one sessions to address student career goals, resolve doubts, and showcase the practical value of the training program.
  • Step 4: High Conversion Rate — Transformed cold social media traffic into paying institute students with minimal drop-off, maintaining a low Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).
